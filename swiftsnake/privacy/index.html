Privacy Policy

Swift Snake

Swift Snake does not collect any data.

Details

  • No data collection

    Swift Snake does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

  • What stays on your device

    Your settings, best score, and any paused game are saved only on your device so the app can remember them. This information never leaves your devices and is not sent to us or anyone else.

  • Syncing with Apple Watch

    If Sync with Apple Watch is turned on, your settings (such as theme and difficulty) are sent directly between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch using Apple's built-in WatchConnectivity framework. No servers of ours are involved.

  • Third parties

    Swift Snake does not include any third-party services, SDKs, or advertising networks.

  • Children

    Because Swift Snake collects no data, it collects no information from children.

  • Changes to this policy

    If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date below.

  • Questions

    If you have any questions about this policy, please visit the Support page.
